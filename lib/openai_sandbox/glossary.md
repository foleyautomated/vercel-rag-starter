## High-Level Concepts

**Dimensionality**: What order of complexity does the data have?

- Higher Dimensional Space: Words/Images
- Lower Dimensional Space: Arrays and such
- Dimensionality Reduction: In the context of Natural Language Processing, this is the process of reducing the **Dimensionality** of data while retaining key information

**Embedding** - **Lower Dimensional** representation of **Higher Dimensional** Data

**Semantic Representation**: The semantic relationship between words. "Apple" and "Orange" will be relatively close.

## Strategies

**Retrieval Augmented Generation** (RAG): Normal Text generation, but with context.
**Cosine Similarity**: a method for determining the semantic similarity between two **embeddings**
**Chunking**: The process of breaking down large pieces of data into smaller, more manageable pieces or "chunks" for easier processing and analysis. Think, sentences pulled from a paragraph.
